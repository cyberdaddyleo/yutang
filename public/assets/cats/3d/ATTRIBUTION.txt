Modifications for this project: normalized scale and ground placement; newly authored quadruped skeleton and heat skin weights; four-foot IK controls; baked Idle, Walk, Rest, Sleep, Eat, Drink, Groom, Scratch, Play, Pet, HopUp and HopDown actions; head-bound eye and nose geometry; breed morphology and eyelid morph targets; sixteen procedural coats and skinned fur tufts. Original continuous body mesh retained. The public source FBX contained no armature, animations or painted coat textures. All coats and rig/action changes above are authored for this project; the supplied original portraits remain separate reference art.

courtyard-cat.glb is the production asset. pilot-cat.glb is the earlier technical review asset, retained for provenance and rollback. No Blendkit model or restricted Blendkit texture is included in either asset.
